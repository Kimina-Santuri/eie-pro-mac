How to install EIE Pro for Mac

This makes the Akai EIE Pro work on modern Macs. You need a Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer, Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3…) or Intel. It takes about 5 minutes.

1. Open the installer

Double-click EIE Pro @VERSION@.pkg (in the folder this note came in).

macOS will probably say it could not verify the installer. That is normal for free software that isn't sold through Apple. Click Done (not "Move to Trash").

2. Allow it once

  1. Open System Settings (Apple menu at the top left → System Settings).
  2. Click Privacy & Security in the list on the left.
  3. Scroll down to Security. You'll see a line saying the EIE Pro installer was blocked. Click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click Open.

3. Install

Click Continue, Agree, then Install, and enter your Mac password. Near the end your Mac's sound stops for a second: that is macOS loading the driver. Click Close when it's done.

A small round waveform icon appears at the top right of the screen, in the menu bar. That is the EIE Pro app.

4. Plug in the EIE Pro

Connect the EIE Pro with its USB cable and switch it on. When the Mac sees it, the menu-bar icon fills in and its menu says Firmware 1.04 (or similar).

5. Choose the EIE Pro

  • For all Mac sound: System Settings → Sound → Output: EIE Pro (and Input: EIE Pro to record).
  • Ableton Live: Settings → Audio → Audio Input Device and Audio Output Device: EIE Pro.
  • Other music apps: pick EIE Pro in their audio settings.

The first time an app records from the EIE Pro, macOS asks whether it may use the microphone. Click Allow, or the app will record silence.

The menu-bar app

  • Sample rate: 44.1k, 48k, 88.2k or 96k.
  • Meters: the four inputs and four outputs.
  • Keep the box awake: stops the small tick when sound starts, and lets MIDI work when nothing is playing.
  • MIDI: the EIE Pro's MIDI sockets appear in music apps as EIE Pro MIDI In and EIE Pro MIDI Out.
  • Open at login: starts the app when you log in, so the MIDI ports are always there.

If something doesn't work

  • EIE Pro doesn't appear: unplug the USB cable, wait 5 seconds, plug it back in. Check the box is switched on.
  • No sound: check System Settings → Sound → Output is EIE Pro, and the EIE Pro's volume knobs are up.
  • The needle meters barely move: the music is quiet. Turn up the song, the app or the faders.
  • All Mac sound stops working: restart the Mac.

To remove it

Click the waveform icon in the menu bar → Uninstall… → Uninstall, and enter your Mac password.
