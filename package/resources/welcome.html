EIE Pro for Mac

A free driver that makes the Akai EIE Pro work on modern Macs (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or newer): 4 inputs, 4 outputs, 44.1 to 96 kHz.

This installs two things:

  • The EIE Pro driver, so the EIE Pro shows up in Sound settings, Ableton Live and other music apps.
  • The EIE Pro app, a small icon in the menu bar with input and output meters, the sample rate and MIDI.

At the end, your Mac's sound stops for a second while macOS reloads its audio. Plug the EIE Pro in any time.

To remove it later: click the EIE Pro icon in the menu bar, then Uninstall…
